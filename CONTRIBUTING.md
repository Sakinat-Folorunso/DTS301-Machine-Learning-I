# Working with the DTS 301 Repository

## For students

1. Open the relevant week.
2. Choose your programming language:
   - Python
   - R
3. Follow the notebook or R Markdown guide in sequence.
4. Complete the Lab Task.
5. Complete the Do It Yourself section.
6. Complete the reflection.
7. Save your work separately so that your original course material remains unchanged.

## Python

Python materials are designed for Jupyter Notebook and Google Colab.

## R

R materials are designed for RStudio and R Markdown.

## Academic practice

Students should write explanations and interpretations in their own words and should be able to explain the code they submit.
